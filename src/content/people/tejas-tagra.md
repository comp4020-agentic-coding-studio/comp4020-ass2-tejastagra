Associate on the Investment Team at Significant Ventures, where the actual
reply-to-cold-email ratio is a matter of professional record. Founded and ran
the ANU Entrepreneurship Club, which meant reading a great many pitches that
opened with "I hope this finds you well."

Built the taxonomy this course teaches after noticing that the emails worth
replying to and the ones deleted on sight split cleanly on about four
variables, and none of them were charisma. This course is that observation,
stretched across twelve weeks.

Office hours are by email; expect a same-day reply during term, which is
either a good sign for the course's own thesis or a bad one for how much else
is getting read that day.

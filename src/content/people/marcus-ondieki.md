Spent nine years building and running outbound sales teams, most recently as
Head of Outbound at Ledger Metrics, a B2B analytics vendor, where his team
sent something in the region of forty thousand cold emails a quarter and he
personally reviewed a meaningful fraction of them before they went out.

Left that role once the honest answer to "why isn't this sequence working"
stopped being about copy and started being about the fact that nobody
on the list had a reason to open it. Now works in outbound enablement,
auditing other companies' sequences for the same problem.

Reads a rewrite the way he used to read a rep's draft: skims the subject
line, jumps to the ask, checks whether the two agree with each other. Marks
harder on vague asks than on anything else, on the theory that a rep who
can't price the ask hasn't finished thinking about what they want.

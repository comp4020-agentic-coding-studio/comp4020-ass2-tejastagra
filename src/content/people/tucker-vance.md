Masters student studying a Master of Business. Secured sponsorship for
a university club by cold-emailing an old university donor who had
stopped giving because of macro constraints. He found him by reading
an old university magazine and noticing that the scholarship in the
donor's name had been discontinued. He impressed the donor enough that
a new private scholarship was built off the back of it. From a cold
email to a private scholarship.

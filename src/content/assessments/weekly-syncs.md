A weekly sync is like a tutorial. Each teaching week has a short set of
exercises tied to it, and you work through them here, in a structured
in-class setting, rather than on your own. You can find each week's task
under that week on the Weekly Syncs tab.

There are three weekly sync sessions each week, all covering the same
task at different times, so you can pick whichever fits your timetable.
Times and locations are on the course home page.

The tasks are short. You are expected to have watched the lecture or
read through that week's slides before you come, since the sync builds
on that rather than repeating it.

## What happens if you miss one

If you miss a weekly sync, it does not count towards your 8 of 12. There
is no informal way to make it up. The only route is the University's
extenuating circumstances process (an ECA), described on the
[policies page](/policies/). If your application is approved, that
week's sync is set aside and does not count against the 8-of-12
requirement. If it is not approved, the sync counts as missed.

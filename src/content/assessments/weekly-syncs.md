## The brief

> Turn up to your weekly sync, follow your tutor's lead, and do what that
> week's page asks. Twelve of these make up just under half your mark.

Each week has a one-hour Weekly Sync run by one of the three tutors. You
attend one of the three times on offer. Each sync is worth 4% of your
final mark, and that 4% is made up of two separate marks:

- **2% for attending** and following your tutor's lead during the hour.
- **2% for completing** the work set out on that week's Weekly Sync page.

Both marks are recorded through Canvas. Across the twelve weeks, this adds
up to 48% of your final mark, so keeping up with them matters as much as
any single piece of written work.

## What happens if you miss one

If you miss a weekly sync, you lose both marks for that week. There is no
informal way to make this up. The only route is the University's
extenuating circumstances process (an ECA), described on the
[policies page](/policies/). If that application is approved, the missed
marks are handled under that process; if it isn't, the marks are simply
lost.

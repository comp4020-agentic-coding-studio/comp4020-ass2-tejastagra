## What happens if you miss one

If you miss a weekly sync, you lose both marks for that week. There is no
informal way to make this up. The only route is the University's
extenuating circumstances process (an ECA), described on the
[policies page](/policies/). If your application is approved, that week's
sync is dropped from your mark and your remaining syncs are reweighted to
cover it, so the missed week does not count against you. If it is not
approved, the marks are lost.

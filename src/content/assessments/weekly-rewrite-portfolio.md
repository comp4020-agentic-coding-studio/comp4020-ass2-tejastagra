## The brief

> Fix one named failure per week, and show your working.

Each week names one specific way a cold email fails. This portfolio is where
that diagnosis gets applied to real writing rather than left as a concept:
find an email with the week's failure mode, and rewrite it so the failure is
actually gone, not just softened.

The room this brief leaves is in the diagnosis itself — which real emails you
choose, and whether your account of what was wrong with them holds up.
Correcting the wrong problem, or fixing the tone without fixing the failure
mode, is graded as a miss even if the rewrite reads better.

## What you submit

Six rewrite entries, weeks 1 through 6, each with: the original email
(yours or someone else's, redacted), the rewritten version, and a short note
— a few sentences, not an essay — on what specifically changed and why it
addresses that week's failure mode rather than some other improvement.

Submitted as part of your site repository, alongside your `PROCESS.md`.

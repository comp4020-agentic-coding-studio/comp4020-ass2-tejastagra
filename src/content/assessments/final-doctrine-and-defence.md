## What you submit

A one-page doctrine, and a defence of one real email (yours, redacted if
needed) run against it. Submitted through Canvas under the Assignments
page.

## What a good submission looks like

A real doctrine argues for something you could be wrong about, not a
summary of the twelve weeks in your own words. Finding real tension in
the defence means choosing an email you would genuinely send and naming
at least one place it falls short of your own doctrine, rather than
picking one that obviously clears every bar. A defence with no tension
against the doctrine reads as unexamined, not as a clean pass.

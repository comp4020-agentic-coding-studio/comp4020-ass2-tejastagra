## The brief

> Given ten cold emails, say exactly why each one fails, and what would fix
> it.

You are given ten redacted real cold emails. For each one, explain what is
wrong with it and why, using what the course has taught so far, and point
at the specific line or structural choice responsible rather than giving a
general impression of quality.

This tests whether you can find the problems yourself, not just fix ones
you have already been handed.

## What you submit

Ten entries, each with: the email (redacted), a short explanation of what
is wrong and why, and one sentence naming the fix. Submitted as part of
your site repository.

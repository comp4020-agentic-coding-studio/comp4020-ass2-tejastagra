## What you submit

Ten redacted real cold emails, each with a short explanation of what is
wrong with it and why (pointing at the specific line or structural choice
responsible, not a general impression), and one sentence naming the fix.
Submitted through Canvas under the Assignments page.

## What a good submission looks like

One entry done well looks like this:

Email: "Hi, hope you're well. I'd love to pick your brain sometime about
your career path, would you be free for a quick call?"

Problem: the second sentence asks for an open-ended call with no stated
topic or length, so the reader has to define the ask before they can even
agree to it.

Fix: name the specific question and cap the time, for example "Could you
spare 15 minutes to tell me how you moved from engineering into product?"

Ten entries built this way, each grounded in the email's own wording
rather than a general impression, is a pass.

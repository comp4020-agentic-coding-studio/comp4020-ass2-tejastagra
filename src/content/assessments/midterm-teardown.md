## The brief

> Given ten cold emails, say exactly why each one fails, and what would fix
> it.

This is the diagnostic half of the skill the portfolio exercises the
corrective half of. Given ten redacted real cold emails, assign each one
the single failure mode from weeks 1 through 7 that best explains why it
would be ignored, and justify that call by pointing at the specific line or
structural choice responsible — not a general impression of quality.

## What you submit

Ten entries, each with: the email (redacted), the assigned failure mode, a
two-to-three sentence justification pointing at specific text, and one
sentence naming the fix. Submitted as part of your site repository.

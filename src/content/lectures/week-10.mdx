## In brief

This week's failure is manipulative craft: the same techniques taught all
semester, tipped past the point of being honest about what the reader is
looking at. A real deadline becomes an invented one; personalisation built
at scale simulates a relationship that doesn't exist. It matters because
the technique itself doesn't change, only whether the reader would still
believe it if they knew exactly how it was produced, and that's the test
this lecture asks students to apply to their own work.

## Outline

- fabricated deadlines vs. real ones
- personalisation at scale vs. simulated relationship
- the exact sentence where craft becomes manipulation
- a test for the line: would the reader still believe it, knowing how it
  was made

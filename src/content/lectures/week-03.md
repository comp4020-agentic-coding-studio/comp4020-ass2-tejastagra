Covers why an unpriced ask costs the reader more than a specific one, even
when it sounds more polite, and introduces a three-question rubric for
checking any ask before sending it.

## Outline

- politeness and cost aren't the same axis
- the three jobs a vague ask quietly hands to the reader
- the three-question rubric: answerable in one sentence, cost known,
  specific next action
- worked example: vague ask vs. priced ask
- how this connects back to week 1's "give the reader enough to decide with"

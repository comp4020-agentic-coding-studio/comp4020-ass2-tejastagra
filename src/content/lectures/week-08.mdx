## In brief

This week's failure is genre confusion between a cold ask and a warm-intro
request. Asking a stranger for ten minutes risks the stranger's time.
Asking a connector to introduce you risks the connector's relationship
with the person on the other end, a cost most writers never mention. It
matters because writing both requests the same way treats the connector's
risk as if it weren't there, when the fix is just naming it and giving
them an easy way to say no.

## Outline

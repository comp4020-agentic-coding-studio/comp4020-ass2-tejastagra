The reader's math from last week doesn't reset when a first email goes
unanswered. It gets asked again. This lecture is about what a second
email needs to contain to justify asking it a second time.

</Callout>

## In brief

This week's failure is the costly follow-up: "just bumping this up" makes
the reader pay the original cost of attention again, with none of the
original content's chance of being useful. A follow-up earns its place
only by adding something the first email didn't have, a new fact, a
narrower ask, a deadline that's now real. It matters because persistence
isn't free for the reader even when it's free for the sender, so a follow-
up with nothing new just spends the goodwill the first email built.

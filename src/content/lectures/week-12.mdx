## In brief

This week's failure is legal noncompliance: sending unsolicited commercial
email without the required disclosures, emailing addresses you never had
permission to hold, or ignoring an unsubscribe request. It sits in a
different category from every earlier week, "this got ignored" versus
"this was illegal to send," and it closes the semester because it tends to
trigger at the exact point good craft turns into volume. The synthesis for
the final assessment follows from that: legal compliance and craft fail
independently, so passing one says nothing about the other.

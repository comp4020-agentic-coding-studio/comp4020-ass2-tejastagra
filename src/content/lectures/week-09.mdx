Every failure named so far assumed email's particular constraints: a
subject line, no read receipt, an inbox the reader chose to check. This
lecture asks what happens when the same message moves somewhere else.

## In brief

This week's failure is platform mismatch: copying a cold email into a
LinkedIn message or a DM and expecting it to read the same way it would
in an inbox. A LinkedIn message gets skimmed inside a feed the reader
didn't choose to open right now, and a DM sits inside a channel with its
own expectations about how fast people reply. It matters because length
tolerance and follow-up cadence both change across platforms, so a message
that would pass in email can fail purely on where it landed.

## Outline

- skimming constraints specific to email, feeds, and DM threads
- which failure modes carry across platforms unchanged, and which don't
- what has to change beyond length: tone, cadence, acceptable asks
- adapting one message across three platforms

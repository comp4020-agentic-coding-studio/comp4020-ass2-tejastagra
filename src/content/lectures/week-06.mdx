Weeks 2 through 5 named individual craft failures. This lecture steps back
and gives the reader's decision an actual shape: every opened email gets
weighed against what replying costs, and most cold email is optimised for
the sender's side of that weighing and never touches the reader's. The
full workthrough is on the slide deck linked above.

## In brief

This week's failure is having no reply incentive: an email that costs the
reader real time to answer while offering them nothing back for spending
it. The lecture names a rough framework, relevance times specificity
divided by length, for finding which term is actually weak before you
rewrite anything. It matters because writers usually fix the term that's
easiest to fix rather than the one that's actually broken, and a rewrite
that targets the wrong term wastes the effort.

## In brief

This week names performed personalisation: a line that mentions the reader
without committing to anything that would be false for a different reader.
"I loved your recent post on X" could go to anyone who posted recently, so
it isn't specificity, whatever name it drops. The tell is almost always
the second sentence, where a template has nothing left to say and either
goes vague or pivots straight to the pitch. It matters because a reader
who spots the pattern reads the rest of the email as mass-produced, even
if the pitch underneath it is genuine.

## Exercise: sort the sentences (30 min)

Take an email you've written that felt necessary at every sentence. Go
through it line by line and sort each sentence into load-bearing or
throat-clearing, without cutting anything yet.

## Exercise: cut it by half (30 min)

Cut the email by half without losing the ask, using the sort from the
first exercise as your guide. Note whether the ratio of throat-clearing to
load-bearing surprised you.

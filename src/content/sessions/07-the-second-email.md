## Exercise: name the new fact (30 min)

Pick an email of yours that went unanswered. Before drafting anything,
name the one new thing a follow-up would tell the reader that the first
email didn't. If you can't name one, decide whether to wait or drop it.

## Exercise: draft the follow-up (30 min)

Draft the follow-up itself, built around the new fact named in the first
exercise rather than around urgency. Check that removing the new fact
would make the follow-up not worth sending.

## Exercise: score an email (30 min)

Take an email that got no reply. Score it on relevance, specificity, and
length, using the framework from the lecture, and identify which term was
weakest.

## Exercise: rewrite the weakest term (30 min)

Rewrite only the weakest term from the first exercise, changing nothing
else. Compare the two versions side by side and check whether the fix
actually addresses what the scoring found.

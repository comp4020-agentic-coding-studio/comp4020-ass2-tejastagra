Follows the [week 4 lecture](/lectures/week-04/) on the second-sentence
tell.

## Exercise: test a line (30 min)

Find a line of "personalisation" in a cold email addressed to you. Try to
rewrite it so it would also be true, word for word, of ten other
recipients. If you can, it wasn't personalisation.

## Exercise: make it fail the test (30 min)

Write a version of the same line that would be false for anyone else —
committing to a detail specific enough that it couldn't be sent to a
different reader without becoming wrong.

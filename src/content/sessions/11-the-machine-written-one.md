## Exercise: build the template (30 min)

Take a one-to-one email you're proud of and try to turn it into a template
with two variables. Mark which sentences survive the merge without change.

## Exercise: audit what broke (30 min)

For every sentence that didn't survive the merge, write down exactly why:
what made it true of one specific reader and false, or empty, once
templated.

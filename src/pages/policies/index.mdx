# Policies and support

<Callout title="Getting help">
  Talk to your tutor in the weekly sync in the first instance. If something
  doesn't feel right, drop us an email at SLOP3089@slop.university.
</Callout>

## Late submissions

Late submissions are not accepted. You need a previously approved extension.
To request one, email the course at SLOP3089@slop.university before the
deadline. Documentation is not required in the first instance.

All assignments are submitted through Canvas. Do not submit work by email.
If you need to make a hard-copy submission, get in touch with the Associate
Dean of Education of the Communication Slop College at Slop University.

## Extenuating circumstances

If you need an extension of more than 10 working days, submit an
Extenuating Circumstances Application (ECA) through the University's Exams,
Graduations and Prizes office. That process is not managed by the course
convenor.

## Academic integrity

You must submit a declaration that all graded work in this course is your
own. You are permitted to use generative AI, but any such use must comply
with the University's academic integrity policy and rules. It is better to
risk poor marks on an assignment than to breach the academic integrity
rules, because the consequences are serious. If you use anyone else's work,
you must cite it in ACM format.

## Discrimination and harassment

This course has zero tolerance for discrimination and harassment. All
conduct is subject to the University's discipline rules and the Student
Code of Conduct.

# Policies and support

<Callout title="Getting help">
  Check Canvas first. If you still need help, talk to your tutor in the
  weekly sync. If something doesn't feel right, drop us an email at
  SLOP3089@slop.university.
</Callout>

## Late submissions

Late submissions are not accepted. You need a previously approved extension.
To request one, email the course at SLOP3089@slop.university before the
deadline. Documentation is not required in the first instance.

All assignments are submitted through Canvas. Do not submit work by email.
If you need to make a hard-copy submission, get in touch with the Associate
Dean of Education of the Communication Slop College at Slop University.

## Extenuating circumstances

Extensions of up to 10 working days are handled by the course: email
SLOP3089@slop.university before the deadline. For an extension of more than
10 working days, submit an Extenuating Circumstances Application (ECA)
through the University's Exams, Graduations and Prizes office. That process
is not managed by the course convenor.

## Feedback and return dates

Feedback for an assessment is returned within 14 days of submitting it. If
you submit an assessment late on an approved extension, your feedback may
come back later than the rest of the class.

## Appealing your result

There are two ways to raise a concern about a result.

Informally, if you think a result is wrong, raise it with us first by
emailing SLOP3089@slop.university. The tutor who marked or responded to
your work will get back to you.

Formally, you cannot appeal the result of a single assessment on its own.
A formal appeal is against your overall result for the course, not an
individual assessment. You have 20 working days to lodge one, and the
process follows the University's Coursework policies and procedures. See
Extenuating circumstances above if the issue is about a missed or late
submission rather than a result.

## Academic integrity

You must submit a declaration that all graded work in this course is your
own. You are permitted to use generative AI, but any such use must comply
with the University's academic integrity policy and rules. It is better to
risk poor marks on an assignment than to breach the academic integrity
rules, because the consequences are serious. If you use anyone else's work,
you must cite it in ACM format.

## Discrimination and harassment

This course has zero tolerance for discrimination and harassment. All
conduct is subject to the University's discipline rules and the Student
Code of Conduct.

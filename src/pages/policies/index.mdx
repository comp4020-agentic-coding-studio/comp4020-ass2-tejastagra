# Policies and support

## Policy on generated text

This course teaches you to notice the specific, generic voice a language
model defaults to when writing cold email — that recognition is most of what
weeks 4 and 11 are for. Submitting a Weekly Rewrite Portfolio entry that is
itself unedited model output, without disclosing it, undermines the exact
skill the assessment is checking for.

Using a model as part of your process is not the issue, and is not
prohibited. Submitting its raw output as your own diagnosis or your own
rewrite, undisclosed, is. If a portfolio entry, teardown diagnosis, or the
final doctrine draws on generated text, say so in the entry itself — a
sentence is enough.

## Getting help

Use the weekly session for feedback on rewrites and diagnoses in progress.
Email the instructor for questions about the course itself; email or message
the tutor for marking questions on work already submitted.
